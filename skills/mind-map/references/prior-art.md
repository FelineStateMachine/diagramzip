# Mind-map references

- [Tony Buzan’s mind map guidance](https://www.tonybuzan.com/about/mind-mapping/) — prior art for a central image, radiating branches, keywords, and visual association.
- [Mermaid mindmap syntax](https://mermaid.js.org/syntax/mindmap.html) — supported diagram.zip renderer syntax; check current renderer behavior before relying on experimental features.

Mind maps have no single ISO/OMG interchange standard. A standards-based taxonomy or concept map may be a better fit when formal semantics are required.
